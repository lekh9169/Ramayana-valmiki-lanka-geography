Two timelines, one dataset

The same bathymetry, Sanskrit text and astronomy are read twice. The historical edition follows the text's own month–season clues to the last few thousand years. The Treta-Yuga edition takes the Puranic chronology at its word and recomputes the sky and the sea for about 870,000 years ago.

What the data show

  • Adam's Bridge is the only shallow link between India and Sri Lanka. Its crest lies 0–3 m below today's sea, and at the −4 m depth line it is about ten times longer than it is wide, as Valmiki's 100 × 10 yojanas says.
  • The sea rose over the bridge about 5219 BCE. Before that Sri Lanka was part of India and no causeway was needed.
  • Rama's horoscope with five exalted planets cannot occur on any date. The ninth lunar day with the Moon in Punarvasu places the Sun in late Pisces, outside its exaltation sign.
  • The month–season pairings of the Critical Edition hold only from about 2951 BCE onward. Older proposed dates put Chaitra in winter.
  • At the end of Treta the sea stood about 90 m lower. The Palk Bay was dry land and the Tamraparni ran some 30 km further to the sea.
  • The Surya Siddhanta's equatorial "Lanka", on Ujjain's meridian, lies under 4,308 m of ocean and was never land.
  • A limestone island of about 14,551 km² once stood on the Great Chagos Bank. Rising sea level drowned it between about 10,000 and 8,000 years ago.
  • Valmiki's distances imply a yojana anywhere from 0.3 km to 28 km. The text's numbers are poetic, not surveyed.
