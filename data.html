Data

Everything behind the two editions, ready to open in Google Earth, a GIS or a spreadsheet. Coordinates are WGS84 decimal degrees.

Rebuild everything

The scripts folder holds the full pipeline. With Python, numpy, scipy, matplotlib, jplephem and rebound installed, and the GEBCO 2026 GeoTIFF on disk, run python scripts/run_all.py. Steps that need the JPL ephemerides (DE441, 1.65 GB; DE440s, 32 MB) are skipped if those files are absent, and the saved results are reused.

Bathymetry and land elevation: GEBCO Compilation Group (2026) GEBCO 2026 Grid (doi: 10.5285/4f68d5c7-45eb-f999-e063-7086abc036fa).
