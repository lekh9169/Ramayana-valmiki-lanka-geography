Map of the places

Both editions on one map. Blue markers are today's identifications of places named in the text. Ochre shows the Treta-Yuga world: the shore and coastline at a sea level about 90 m lower, the equatorial candidates and the drowned banks of Chagos. Select any marker for its verse and coordinates.

Marker shade shows confidence: darker for a direct textual fit, greyer for a weaker one. Coordinates are WGS84. The base map is for orientation only; every computed layer comes from GEBCO 2026.
